Frequently Asked Questions

Common technical and research inquiries answered by AI

Precision agriculture is a management concept based on observing, measuring, and responding to inter- and intra-field variability in crops using GPS, sensors, and AI to optimize resource use.
AI assists farming through image recognition for disease detection, predictive weather modeling, field monitoring via satellite analysis, and automated irrigation control.
Soil erosion is primarily caused by water runoff, strong winds, overgrazing, deforestation, and intensive tillage that breaks down soil structure and removes protective vegetation cover.
Organic fertilizers derive from biological sources (manure, compost) and improve soil microbial health over time, whereas chemical fertilizers provide synthetic concentrated nutrients for immediate plant uptake.
Crop rotation is the practice of growing different types of crops sequentially on the same land to disrupt pest cycles, replenish soil nutrients, and improve overall soil health.
Soil fertility can be improved by adding organic compost, integrating leguminous cover crops for nitrogen fixation, practicing reduced tillage, and maintaining balanced pH levels.
